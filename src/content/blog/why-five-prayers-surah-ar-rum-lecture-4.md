## Surah Ar-Rum, Lecture 4

Verses 16-21 of Surah Ar-Rum discuss various signs of Allah and our remembrance of him. They describe the times in which He should be remembered, and Sheikh Azhar Nasser shares a hadith about why we have five wajib prayers.
