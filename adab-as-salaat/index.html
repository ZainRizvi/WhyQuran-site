Adab as-Salaat

The Disciplines of the Prayer

About This Book

Adab as-Salaat (The Disciplines of the Prayer) is a profound work by Imam Khomeini exploring the inner dimensions and spiritual significance of the Islamic prayer (salaat).

This translation aims to make these important teachings accessible to English-speaking audiences.
